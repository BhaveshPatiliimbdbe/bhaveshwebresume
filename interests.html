Student Perspective Essay

The Golden Decade of Indian Wellness Brands

Why the convergence of digital access and health consciousness is creating an unprecedented moment for functional consumer products in India.

For generations, India's agricultural riches—from moringa and turmeric to ashwagandha and millets—were largely exported as raw bulk commodities or consumed in unstandardized, rustic forms. As a consequence, younger urban consumers gravitated toward imported Western health supplements and snacks.

Today, a profound shift is underway. Consumers demand transparency, clean ingredient labels, and native superfoods tailored to high-paced modern lifestyles. The brands that win will not merely market ingredients; they will solve format and taste challenges.

Through my studies at IIM Bangalore and building Mira Herbals, I see firsthand that the competitive moat of tomorrow's FMCG brand lies in its connection with the customer. Digital channels allow instant feedback, agile formulation iterations, and genuine community building.

This intersection of deep agricultural pride, consumer empathy, and digital agility is where I aspire to dedicate my entrepreneurial career.
