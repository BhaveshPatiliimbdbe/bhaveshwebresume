The Background

Bridging Commercial Rigor with Creative Curiosity

I have always been fascinated by how products make their way from raw agricultural harvests to supermarket shelves and modern direct-to-consumer pantries.

My academic journey took root in Commerce at Swami Vivekanand Vidyalaya, where I graduated with an 88% aggregate. Studying financial accounting, business dynamics, and microeconomics wasn't merely an academic exercise for me—it laid the quantitative foundation for evaluating balance sheets, cost of goods sold (COGS), and consumer price elasticity.

Entering the BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore was a pivotal milestone. Surrounded by leading management faculty, ambitious peers, and cutting-edge digital frameworks, I realized that waiting for graduation before testing business concepts was a missed opportunity. Real entrepreneurial acumen is formed when classroom theory collides with real-world customer feedback.

The Project

Why Moringa? The Genesis of Mira Herbals

India is the world's largest producer of moringa (drumstick tree), a miraculous plant packed with 7 times the Vitamin C of oranges, 4 times the calcium of milk, and all nine essential amino acids. Yet despite its ancient Ayurvedic heritage, modern urban consumers rarely incorporate it into their busy routines due to bitter taste profiles and archaic branding.

With Mira Herbals, I embarked on a journey to transform moringa from a niche herbal remedy into an indispensable consumer superfood. By managing sourcing, learning packaging standards, running digital marketing campaigns on Facebook, and actively talking to early buyers, I've developed a profound respect for customer discovery and unit economics.

Currently, I am exploring the R&D for a Moringa Chocolate Protein Bar—a functional on-the-go snack designed to overcome the natural earthiness of moringa with pure cocoa and balanced plant protein.

Mindset & Philosophy

How I Approach Work & Learning

Core beliefs that guide my daily academic dedication at IIM Bangalore and my entrepreneurial ventures.

01 Action Orientation

Execution Over Pure Hypothesis

Ideas are abundant; execution is rare. Whether running a pilot Meta ad campaign with modest budgets or packaging moringa batches, I value learning from live market signals over prolonged theoretical debates.

02 Customer Centricity

Deep Empathy for the End User

Every product flaw is an unspoken customer friction. Listening closely to customer feedback regarding taste, packaging convenience, and pricing is the bedrock of building lasting brand trust.

03 Continuous Learning

Learning Agility & Resourcefulness

As a first-year student, I encounter new questions daily—from food preservation and shelf-life testing to Facebook Ads ROAS optimization. I approach every unknown with humility, rigorous research, and rapid experimentation.

04 Uncompromising Standards

Authenticity & Pure Quality

Consumer wellness requires absolute integrity. Mira Herbals is founded on zero compromises on purity, transparent communication, and genuine health benefits for our community.
